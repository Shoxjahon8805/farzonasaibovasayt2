How cacti survive

Water tanks

A cactus stem is thick and soft inside. It soaks up water after a rain and stores it for months.

Spines, not leaves

Spines are special leaves. They lose less water than flat leaves, shade the stem and keep hungry animals away.

Waxy skin

A thick, waxy coat seals water inside, like a lid on a bottle.

Night breathing

Many cacti open their tiny pores at night, when it is cooler, so less water escapes.

Wide, shallow roots

Roots spread out close to the ground, ready to drink fast when rain finally falls.

Flowers and fruit

Many cacti bloom with bright flowers. Bees, bats and birds visit them, and some fruits are sweet to eat.

Cactus words

Spine
A sharp, hard point that grows from a cactus.
Desert
A very dry place that gets little rain.
Succulent
A plant that stores water in thick stems or leaves.
Photosynthesis
How plants use sunlight to make food.
Pollinator
An animal, like a bee or bat, that carries pollen between flowers.

Grow your own

Ask an adult before you start! Use a pot with holes and special cactus soil. Put it in a sunny window. Water it only when the soil is completely dry, about once every 2 to 3 weeks. Too much water is the number one cactus killer.
